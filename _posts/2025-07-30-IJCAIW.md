I am very happy to announce that we have a paper accepted at [ToM IJCAI 2025](https://tomworkshop.github.io).
Our work will be featured as a **long oral talk** at the workshop.
The paper is titled The Yōkai Learning Environment: Tracking Beliefs Over Space and Time and proposes a new multi-agent RL environment for zero-shot coordination and Theory of Mind research.
