Our paper [The Yōkai Learning Environment: Tracking Beliefs Over Space and Time](https://www.collaborative-ai.org/publications/ruhdorfer26_rlc/) has been accepted at **RLC 2026**. Yōkai is a benchmark for coordination with unfamiliar partners that requires tracking beliefs over space and time.
